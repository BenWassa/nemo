# Style & Language Guide

## 1) Voice & Tone

* **Voice:** cinematic, lucid, quietly confident.
* **Tone:** mythic but grounded; warm, not academic; evocative without purple prose.
* **Stance:** guide, not lecturer—invite, don’t declare.

## 2) Reading Level & Clarity

* **Target:** Grade 8–10 readability.
* **Sentences:** 8–18 words on average. Vary rhythm (short punch + medium + occasional longer for cadence).
* **Paragraphs:** 2–4 sentences. Keep air in the layout.
* **Jargon:** minimal. If a term is needed (e.g., “archetype”), define once, then use lightly.

## 3) Language Choices

* **Verbs:** concrete and active (e.g., “grips,” “loosens,” “yields,” “summons”).
* **Nouns:** precise and sensory (“current,” “reef,” “net,” “mask,” “scar”).
* **Metaphor:** sparing, clean, thematically relevant (sea, light, current, depth). One image per paragraph max.
* **Archetypes:** capitalize sparingly for emphasis (Tragic Father, Wounded Son, Divine Helper). Don’t stack more than 2–3 per section.

## 4) Structure & Sections (per episode/article)

* **Opening (2–3 sentences):** sensory vignette that sets the emotional register.
* **Beat Summary (3–5 bullets):** orient without recapping; only the moments you’ll analyze.
* **Analysis (3 short subsections):**

  * *Theme:* 1–2 lines, plain language.
  * *Archetypes:* name + why it matters.
  * *Psychology:* the shift/tension in one clean sentence.
* **Show Notes (3–6 bullets):** quotable takeaways; cross-links to characters/themes.
* **(Optional) Practice/Prompt:** if included, 1 line, actionable.

## 5) Pacing & Emphasis

* **Rule:** “one idea per breath.”
* Use **bold** for the single key term per bullet (e.g., **Flow vs. Force**).
* Prefer lists over dense paragraphs.
* End sections on a clear turn (e.g., “control loosens”).

## 6) What to Avoid

* Academic hedging (“arguably,” “in many ways”).
* Over-explaining plot.
* Stacked metaphors or mixed imagery.
* Therapy-speak clichés (“hold space,” “inner child”) unless directly relevant and reframed.
