# Episode Content Skeletons

---

## Episode 1 — *The Wound*

**Opening**

* Establish the emotional tone: light reef → sudden shattering.
* Convey Marlin’s vow: love fused with fear.

**Beat Summary**

* The barracuda attack and loss.
* Marlin’s overprotection of Nemo.
* Nemo’s small rebellion at school.
* Nemo’s capture and disappearance.

**Analysis**

* *Theme:* Trauma reshapes love into control.
* *Archetypes:* Tragic Father, Wounded Son, Lost Innocence.
* *Psychological arc:* From brittle certainty → rupture → reluctant pursuit.

**Show Notes (Key Elements)**

* The scar and the vow.
* Overprotective parenting as armor.
* “Touching the boat” as a symbol of autonomy.
* The mask clue: grief becomes pursuit.

---

## Episode 2 — *Road of Trials*

**Opening**

* Introduce the idea of unexpected teachers.
* Tone: comic hazards carrying hidden wisdom.

**Beat Summary**

* Marlin meets Dory: forgetfulness as strange ally.
* Encounters with sharks and the anglerfish.
* Nemo’s life in the dentist’s tank; meeting Gill.

**Analysis**

* *Theme:* Help arrives mis-labeled — wisdom in flawed companions.
* *Archetypes:* Reluctant Hero, Wise Mentor, Threshold Guardians.
* *Psychological arc:* Fear loosens its grip; trust enters cautiously.

**Show Notes (Key Elements)**

* Dory’s “just keep swimming” as presence.
* The shark pledge: appetite under covenant.
* Gill’s scar as a badge of earned wisdom.
* Language as lifeline: deciphering the mask.

---

## Episode 3 — *Deepening Crisis*

**Opening**

* Set tone: fatigue, overwhelm, and unexpected relief.
* Shift from strain to surrender in the current.

**Beat Summary**

* Jellyfish ordeal: play turns to peril.
* Rescue and entry into the East Australian Current.
* Crush’s parenting by release.
* Nemo’s escape attempts in the tank.

**Analysis**

* *Theme:* The difference between forcing and aligning.
* *Archetypes:* Shadow Tests, Shapeshifter, Zen Mentor.
* *Psychological arc:* Exhaustion cracks control; ease is discovered in flow.

**Show Notes (Key Elements)**

* Jellyfish field as test of bond.
* The EAC: strength in surrender.
* Crush’s mentorship: trust without gripping.
* Tank scheme: practicing courage through trial and error.

---

## Episode 4 — *Ordeal & Transformation*

**Opening**

* Tone: vast, ego-breaking confrontation with the unknown.
* Symbolic setting: swallowed by the whale, tested in the harbor.

**Beat Summary**

* Inside the whale: surrender as the only path.
* Arrival in Sydney harbor.
* Marlin and Nemo’s reunion attempt.
* The fishing net sequence and communal salvation.

**Analysis**

* *Theme:* Transformation through surrender and death–rebirth.
* *Archetypes:* Transformed Hero, Divine Helper, Communal Will.
* *Psychological arc:* From resistance → surrender → transformation.

**Show Notes (Key Elements)**

* Whale: intelligence greater than self, requires trust.
* The misfired reunion before recognition.
* Net sequence: salvation by coordinated trust.
* Shift in voice: “I know I can” replaces prohibition.

---

## Episode 5 — *Integration & Return*

**Opening**

* Tone: gentle resolution, integrated self.
* Setting: the reef as unchanged world, but changed heart.

**Beat Summary**

* Return to the reef.
* Marlin’s new way of parenting at school send-off.
* Dory’s inclusion in the family circle.

**Analysis**

* *Theme:* Integration of loss and transformation into everyday life.
* *Archetypes:* Integrated Self, Circle Restored.
* *Psychological arc:* Fear dethroned; love redefined as release.

**Show Notes (Key Elements)**

* The unknotted goodbye: trust replaces clinging.
* Dory’s belonging as kin, not guest.
* The reef as both safe home and open horizon.
* Everyday courage: sustaining change through practice.
